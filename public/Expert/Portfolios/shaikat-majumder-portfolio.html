About

A visual generalist by design, not by accident

Shaikat started on an accountancy path at Chakdaha College, India, completing his Honours degree before choosing to pursue image-making full time. That decision took him to the Speos Photography & Cinema Institute in Paris in 2019, where he earned a diploma in photography and filmmaking.

He now runs VanGogh Productions out of Lisbon, Portugal — a creative studio that handles a brand's visual identity end to end: photography, video, graphic design, content, AI-powered production and the digital marketing that puts it in front of the right audience. On film work specifically, he shoots as cinematographer — behind the camera, framing and lighting the story.

Services

What VanGogh Productions covers

One studio, one point of contact, across every part of a brand's visual output — including the AI workflows now behind a lot of it.

Photography

Food, portrait and event photography, shot for how it will actually be used — menus, ads, delivery apps or social feeds.

Cinematography & video

Brand films, reels and short-form video, shot as cinematographer from framing through to final color.

Graphic design

Menus, social templates, print collateral and visual identity systems built to match a brand's tone.

Content creation

Platform-specific content — reels, carousels, campaigns — built around what each channel actually rewards.

Digital marketing

Positioning, audience research and growth planning behind the content, not just the content itself.

Social media management

Ongoing content calendars, posting and day-to-day community upkeep for restaurant and hospitality clients.

AI & automation

AI content production

AI-assisted image and video generation for campaigns, built on top of real photography and film, not instead of it.

AI avatars

Custom AI avatars and synthetic presenters for brands that need a consistent on-screen face without a full shoot every time.

AI automation

Automating the repetitive parts of content and marketing production — scheduling, resizing, tagging, reporting.

AI agents

Custom AI agents built around a specific business workflow, from client intake to content approval.

Web development

Simple, fast websites for small businesses that need a clean presence online without the overhead.

Print, press & collaborations

Press kits, printed materials and influencer partnerships that extend a campaign beyond the feed.
