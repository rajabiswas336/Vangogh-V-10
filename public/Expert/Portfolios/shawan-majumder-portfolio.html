About

Design that's both attractive and effective

Shawan Majumder is a passionate, detail-oriented graphic designer based in Kalyani, West Bengal, India. He specialises in creating visually appealing, creative, and purpose-driven designs for businesses, brands, events, restaurants and individual clients.

He developed his interest in design through hands-on creative work, gradually building his skills with Adobe Illustrator and other digital design tools. He currently works on freelance and client-based projects — commercial, promotional, restaurant, event and custom design — and is expanding his portfolio toward more professional and international clients.
